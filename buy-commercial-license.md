## 💼 Why Obtain a Commercial License?

Under the AGPLv3 license, any software, driver, firmware, or network service that incorporates or interfaces with HWCode must also make its **entire source code available to users under the AGPLv3**.

If your business or project:
- **Cannot or does not want to disclose proprietary source code**
- Requires embedding HWCode into proprietary silicon, ASICs, FPGAs, NPUs, or embedded firmware
- Needs to distribute proprietary C11 binaries or driver scaffolds generated by the HWCode compiler
- Requires enterprise warranty, legal indemnification, or custom hardware graph driver engineering
- Demands private SLA-backed bug fixes, architectural consulting, and feature prioritizations
